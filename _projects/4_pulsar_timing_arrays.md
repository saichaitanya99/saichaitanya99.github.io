Pulsar timing arrays (PTAs) use the regular signals of millisecond pulsars to search for nanohertz gravitational waves.

I am a member of the European Pulsar Timing Array (since 2021) and the International Pulsar Timing Array (since 2020). From 2018 to 2021 I was a
member of the Indian Pulsar Timing Array, observing with the uGMRT. My contributions include combining the EPTA's second data release with
low-frequency pulsar data.
