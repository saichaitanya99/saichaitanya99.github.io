From 2021 to 2025 I was Chief Observer for the Irish LOFAR station (I-LOFAR), handling its pulsar observations and data processing. From 2023 to
2025 I also scheduled and processed observations from the German long-wavelength LOFAR stations.

I-LOFAR data underpin my work on ecliptic pulsars and the solar wind, as well as studies of individual pulsars and of the station itself. In
summer 2024 I supervised a student project on single pulses of the Crab pulsar with I-LOFAR.
