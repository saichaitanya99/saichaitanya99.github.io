Pulse profiles change shape with observing frequency, and if that frequency evolution is not modelled correctly it biases the times of arrival (ToAs)
used in pulsar timing.

In this project I use **simulation-based inference (SBI)** to perform profile-domain timing, accurately modelling the frequency evolution of pulse
profiles. We are extending the method to the time domain and generalising it to any pulsar, so that a trained model can be applied directly to new
datasets. This makes it possible to disentangle ToA biases caused by other chromatic effects, and to propagate the uncertainties in the profile into
the ToAs, which matters for high-precision timing with pulsar timing arrays.

First results were presented at the International Pulsar Timing Array Meeting 2026 in Drakensberg, South Africa, and at SPINS UK 2026 in Bath.
