Pulsars close to the ecliptic are observed through the solar wind each year as the Sun passes near their line of sight. The extra dispersion this
adds is a source of noise in pulsar timing arrays (PTAs).

My PhD thesis, _Examining ecliptic pulsars using the low-frequency radio telescopes_ (University of Galway, 2021–2025), used low-frequency LOFAR
observations of ecliptic pulsars to study the interstellar medium and the interplanetary medium, in particular the solar wind. The results inform
how the PTA community models timing-residual noise that arises from the heliosphere.

I was principal investigator on two LOFAR Core proposals, _Tracing the Heliosphere with Pulsars_: LC18_016 (10.5 hours, 2022) and LC20_029
(24.5 hours, 2023).
